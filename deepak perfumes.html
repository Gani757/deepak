What goes into the bottle

A flame that doesn't rush.

A diya is small, steady and lights a whole room. That is how we wanted our perfumes to behave: close to the skin at first, then opening slowly through the day.

Every Deepak fragrance starts from the attar traditions of Hyderabad and is finished as a modern eau de parfum you can wear to work, to a wedding, or to nothing at all.

Poured in small batches

Each batch is blended, rested and bottled by hand before it ships.

Built to last

High oil concentration, so a few sprays stay with you from morning to night.
